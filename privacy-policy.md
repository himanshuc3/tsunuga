# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 25, 2026**

Tango ("Tango", "we", "us", or "our") is a Chrome extension designed to help users learn Japanese vocabulary while browsing the web.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Tango collects, how we use it, how it is stored, and when it may be shared.

By using Tango, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Google Account Information

Tango allows users to sign in using Google authentication.

When you sign in with Google, Tango may receive information associated with your Google account, such as:

- Google account identifier
- Email address
- Basic account information necessary to authenticate your account

We use this information to create and authenticate your Tango account and associate your learning data with your account.

We do not use your Google account information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

### 1.2 Learning Data

Tango may collect and store information related to your use of the learning features, including:

- Japanese vocabulary that you save
- Vocabulary review history
- Spaced-repetition information
- Learning progress
- User preferences and extension settings

This information is used to provide and synchronize Tango's learning functionality.

### 1.3 Webpage Content

Tango may access text from webpages when necessary to provide its vocabulary-learning functionality.

For example, when you use Tango to learn Japanese while browsing a webpage, the extension may process Japanese text from that webpage to identify vocabulary and allow you to save words for learning.

Tango does not collect or store your general browsing history for advertising or tracking purposes.

Webpage content accessed by Tango is used only to provide the vocabulary-learning functionality described in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing and user interface.

### 1.4 Locally Stored Information

Tango may store information locally in Chrome's extension storage, including:

- Extension settings
- Cached learning data
- Vocabulary information
- Spaced-repetition state
- Temporary application state

This information allows Tango to function correctly and preserve your preferences between browser sessions.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected information only to provide, maintain, and improve Tango's functionality.

This includes:

- Authenticating your Tango account
- Synchronizing your learning data
- Saving and retrieving vocabulary
- Providing spaced-repetition functionality
- Maintaining your learning progress
- Remembering extension preferences
- Providing support and troubleshooting
- Maintaining the security and reliability of the service

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your information for personalized advertising.

We do not use your browsing activity to create advertising profiles.

## 3. How Information Is Shared

Tango does not sell or rent user information.

Information may be transmitted to services that are necessary to operate Tango, including:

### Google

Google provides the authentication service used to allow users to sign in to Tango.

### Tango's Backend Infrastructure

Tango's backend servers process account and learning information required to provide synchronization and other application functionality.

### Database and Infrastructure Providers

Tango may use third-party infrastructure providers to host its servers and database.

These providers process information only as necessary to provide infrastructure and hosting services for Tango.

We do not share user information with advertising networks, data brokers, or other third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes.

## 4. Google API Data

Tango's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the **Google API Services User Data Policy**, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google account information is used only for authentication and functionality directly related to providing Tango.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data transmitted between the Tango extension and our backend services is transmitted using encrypted HTTPS connections.

Authentication credentials and other sensitive information are protected using appropriate security measures.

However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain account and learning information for as long as necessary to provide Tango's services.

If you delete your Tango account, we will delete or anonymize your associated account and learning data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, security, or legitimate operational purposes.

Information stored locally by the extension may remain on your device until you remove it or uninstall Tango.

## 7. Your Choices and Rights

You may:

- Stop using Tango at any time.
- Sign out of your Tango account.
- Remove the Tango extension from Chrome.
- Request deletion of your Tango account and associated server-side data.
- Request information about the data associated with your account.
